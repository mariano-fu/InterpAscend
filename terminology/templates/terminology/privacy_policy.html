InterpAscend Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2025

InterpAscend (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share when using our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you access https://interpascend.com.

1. Information We Collect

Non-personal data
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • IP address and general location (region/country) for analytics
  • Usage statistics such as pages visited and terms translated
Personal data (if voluntarily provided)
  • Name, email, or message content when you submit a contact form
  • Any additional information you choose to provide when communicating with us

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the InterpAscend website and tools
  • Respond to inquiries, support requests, or feedback
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enable core site functionality
  • Collect analytics data via Google Analytics or similar tools
  • Display relevant ads through Google AdSense once enabled

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of the site may not function properly without them.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect anonymized data about your usage of our site:

  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymized usage statistics to help us understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Google AdSense: May collect data to display relevant advertisements based on your interests

These services operate in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can learn more about how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers like hosting platforms, analytics services, or email processors strictly for site functionality
  • Legal authorities if required to comply with applicable laws or lawful requests

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure hosting provided by Google Cloud to protect your data. However, no method of online transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites (such as dictionaries, learning tools, or references). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

7. Children’s Privacy

InterpAscend is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, such as the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. These rights may include:

  • The right to access your personal data we have collected
  • The right to correct or update inaccurate personal information
  • The right to delete your personal information
  • The right to restrict or object to certain processing activities
  • The right to data portability where applicable

To exercise these rights or for any privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@interpascend.com.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and for legal or security purposes. Specifically:

  • Contact form submissions are retained for up to 2 years or until resolved
  • Usage analytics data may be retained for up to 26 months in accordance with Google Analytics retention policies
  • Non-personal, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytics and service improvement

Data that is no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymized to protect your privacy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Effective date.”

Note for users: InterpAscend translates terminology and does not process or store patient health information (PHI).
